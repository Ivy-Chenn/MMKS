
# 🔧 Troubleshooting

## Overview

This section provides structured troubleshooting resources for Mammotion's distributors, dealers, and authorized service partners.

It covers common product issues, symptom identification, diagnostic procedures, possible causes, and recommended solutions across software, hardware, positioning, navigation, connectivity, and other operational systems.

The goal is to help partners identify issues efficiently, perform appropriate troubleshooting steps, resolve common problems, and determine when further technical support or escalation is required.

## 🛠️ Recommended Troubleshooting Workflow

Follow a structured approach to identify and resolve product issues.

**Step 1 — Identify the Issue**

Confirm the reported symptoms, affected functions, error messages, and conditions under which the issue occurs.

**Step 2 — Verify Product Information**

Check the product model, hardware version, firmware version, App version, and relevant operating conditions.

**Step 3 — Perform Initial Checks**

Inspect the basic operating conditions, power supply, connectivity, installation environment, and other relevant factors.

**Step 4 — Follow Diagnostic Procedures**

Select the appropriate troubleshooting category and perform the recommended checks in sequence.

**Step 5 — Verify the Solution**

Confirm whether the issue has been resolved and whether the robot has returned to normal operation.

**Step 6 — Escalate if Necessary**

If the issue remains unresolved or requires advanced diagnostics, repair, or component replacement, escalate it through the appropriate technical support channel.

## 💡 User Tips

!!! tip "Search by Symptom or Error Code"
    Use the search bar to find troubleshooting information based on error codes, warning messages, product models, or observed symptoms.

!!! tip "Check Basic Conditions First"
    Before performing advanced diagnostics, verify the power supply, network connection, installation conditions, and current software versions.

!!! tip "Follow the Recommended Sequence"
    Complete the applicable diagnostic steps in order. Avoid replacing hardware or performing unnecessary operations before identifying the likely cause.

!!! tip "Collect Relevant Information"
    When escalating an unresolved issue, provide the product model, firmware version, error messages, observed symptoms, troubleshooting steps already performed, and relevant logs or images when available.

    Follow applicable safety instructions before inspection or maintenance. Hardware disassembly, electrical testing, and component replacement should only be performed by appropriately trained and authorized personnel.
