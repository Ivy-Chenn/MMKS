
# Welcome to MMKS Knowledge Base

**MMKS** is a centralized knowledge platform designed for Mammotion's distributors, dealers, and authorized service partners.

It provides structured product information, operational guidelines, technical knowledge, and troubleshooting resources to support partners throughout the product lifecycle — from product selection and customer guidance to installation, diagnostics, and technical service.

The knowledge base supports different partner roles and competency levels (L1–L4), enabling users to access relevant information according to their responsibilities and technical expertise.

---

## 🧭 Quick Navigation

Select a knowledge category below or use the left sidebar to explore the available resources.

### 📦 [Product Info & Setup](PM/index.md)

Explore product specifications, model comparisons, positioning technologies, installation guidelines, and App functions.

**Recommended for:** Product selection, sales consultation, customer onboarding, and installation preparation.

### 🔧 [Troubleshooting](ITR/index.md)

Access issue identification guidelines, diagnostic procedures, error analysis, and recommended solutions covering software, hardware, positioning, navigation, and connectivity.

**Recommended for:** Customer support, remote troubleshooting, and technical issue resolution.

### 📚 Additional Knowledge Areas

As MMKS develops, additional resources will cover technical fundamentals, maintenance procedures, repair guidance, and advanced service knowledge.

## 🎓 Learning Path | L1–L4

MMKS supports a progressive competency framework for external partners, from basic product knowledge to advanced technical service capabilities.

| Level | Knowledge Focus |
|---|---|
| **L1 – Product & Sales** | Product understanding, model selection, basic App operation, and customer guidance |
| **L2 – Installation & Support** | Product setup, installation procedures, operational guidance, and initial issue identification |
| **L3 – Technical Troubleshooting** | System-level understanding, structured diagnostics, and technical issue resolution |
| **L4 – Advanced Technical Service** | Advanced diagnostics, component-level analysis, repair procedures, and complex issue handling |

!!! info "Learning Guidance"
    Knowledge resources may be relevant to multiple competency levels. Users are encouraged to explore topics based on their responsibilities, technical expertise, and service needs.

## 💡 User Tips

!!! tip "Start with Quick Navigation"
    Browse the knowledge categories or use the left sidebar to locate relevant product information, installation instructions, and technical resources.

!!! tip "Use the Search Bar"
    Search by product model, component, error code, symptom, or technical keyword to find related content quickly.

!!! tip "Follow Troubleshooting Procedures"
    Check the applicable product model and follow the recommended diagnostic steps before escalating unresolved issues.

!!! tip "Check Content Applicability"
    Product features, specifications, and procedures may vary depending on the model, hardware version, and firmware version. Always verify that the information applies to the product being serviced.
