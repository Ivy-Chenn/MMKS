#Battery & Charging Troubleshooting

##Auto-Recharge Issue - Vehicle Bumps Station and Exits After Returning, No Charge / Docking Abnormal

**Error Code:**

- 1200 Repeatedly entering/leaving dock / bumping dock
- 2729 Adapter voltage low

**Solution:**

1. Check that the charging station is installed on the ground and that the device's contacts are fully in contact with the charging station's contacts.
2. Check whether the charging contact strips on the rear of the mower and the charging station's probes are dirty or corroded; clean them if necessary.
3. Check whether the connection port between the charging station's power cable and the adapter is loose; re-plug it if necessary.
4. Test the voltage of the charging station contacts and the adapter. If abnormal, send a replacement adapter.

2. Based on the corresponding error code, preliminarily determine the type and possible cause of the robot's fault.

##Battery Issue - Cannot Charge

**Error Code:**

- 2729 Adapter voltage low

**Solution:**

1. For a new device, activate the device first, then dock/undock it on the charging station a few times, confirm that the battery can be charged to 30%, and check that the firmware has been upgraded.
2. If it is not a new device, first clean the charging contact strips on both the mower and the charging station, and check the indicator status of the charging station and the adapter.
3. Check that the charging station is installed on the ground and that the device's contacts are fully in contact with the charging station's contacts.
4. Test the voltage of the charging station contacts and the adapter. If abnormal, send a replacement adapter.
5. After the inspection, try placing the mower on the charging station again to see whether it powers on normally.

##Data Center - Cannot Charge but Data Backend Shows No Abnormality

**Error Code:**

- Adapter fault / probe contact not lit / contact strip corrosion / charging station fault

**Solution:**

1. Check whether the charging station / adapter indicators are normal before and after docking.
2. Check whether the side indicator on the rear of the device is on / slowly flashing after docking.
3. Check whether the device is properly docked. Clean the contact strips on the device / charging station and re-dock for testing.
4. If the issue is resolved, this indicates that a thorough cleaning of the contact strips is required.

##Auto-Recharge Issue - Does Not Recharge Within Mowing Zone

**Error Code:**

- 1201 Stuck
- 1203 Return-to-dock failed
- 1214 Path blocked by obstacle

**Solution:**

1. Check for obstacles around the area. Clear any obstacles to ensure the path is wide enough (LUBA 3: minimum path width is 70cm).
2. If there are no obstacles or clearing them does not help, check whether the LiDAR / vision module is dirty. Clean it and keep it regularly cleaned.
3. If there is no dirt or cleaning does not help, check whether there is a virtual wall / no-go zone blocking the path in the app map.
4. If there is no virtual wall or re-trying does not help, check whether a connecting path is missing between the target zone and other zones. If so, create the path.
