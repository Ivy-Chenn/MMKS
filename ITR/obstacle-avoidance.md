#Obstacle Avoidance Troubleshooting

## **Obstacle Avoidance Abnormal** - Obstacle Avoidance Not Working

**Solution:**

1. Confirm whether the obstacle-avoidance mode has been enabled.
2. If it is already enabled but the issue persists, check whether the abnormal obstacle-avoidance position is on the boundary (LUBA 1 does not avoid obstacles on the boundary position; it is recommended to keep a safe distance).

**Query Page:**

1. Check the Device Profile and Self-check modules to confirm whether the robot's hardware connections are normal.
2. Based on the corresponding error code, preliminarily determine the type and possible cause of the robot's fault.

**If not solved:**
 `Needs FAE Upgrade`

## **Obstacle Avoidance Abnormal** - Drove Under Vehicle

**Solution:**

1. Confirm whether the mower is within the working area. Place the device at least 1m outside the working area, and check whether the issue is resolved.
2. Confirm whether the obstacle-avoidance mode has been enabled.
3. Set the obstacle avoidance to "less touch" in the app and try again.

**Query Page:**

1. Check the Device Profile and Self-check modules to confirm whether the robot's hardware connections are normal.
2. Based on the corresponding error code, preliminarily determine the type and possible cause of the robot's fault.

**If not solved:**
 `Needs FAE Upgrade`

## **Obstacle** - Robot Stuck

**Error Code:**

- 1201/1203 Robot stuck and unable to continue moving

**Solution:**

1. Collect photos of the stuck location and the surrounding environment, the time when the stuck occurred, and whether it always occurs at the same location.
2. If it always occurs at the same location, ensure that the mower does not operate beyond the boundary. The mower should not scrape against the map boundary. Set no-go zones for trees / poles / flower beds to avoid damage, and draw an obstacle-free zone at the stuck location.

**Query Page:**

1. Check the Device Profile and Self-check modules to confirm whether the robot's hardware connections are normal.
2. Based on the corresponding error code, preliminarily determine the type and possible cause of the robot's fault.

**If not solved:**
 `Needs FAE Upgrade`

## **Obstacle** - Path Blocked by Obstacle

**Error Code:**

- 1214 Path blocked by obstacle

**Solution:**

1. Verify the stuck location and the surrounding environment, and confirm whether there are any obvious obstacles.
2. If there are obvious obstacles, set an obstacle-free zone at the stuck location while ensuring that the device does not scrape against the boundary.

## **Bumper Bar** - Bumper Bar Continuously Triggered

**Error Code:**

- 1207 Bumper bar persistently triggered

**Solution:**

1. Check the front bumper assembly: whether the four sides are lifted / visibly loose, whether the cable is connected, whether the screws are installed, etc.
2. After removing the front bumper, check whether error 1207 is still reported.

**Query Page:**

1. Check the Device Profile and Self-check modules to confirm whether the robot's hardware connections are normal.
2. Based on the corresponding error code, preliminarily determine the type and possible cause of the robot's fault.

**If not solved:**
 `Needs Repair`
