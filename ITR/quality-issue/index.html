    Quality Issue Troubleshooting

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Vision Module Damaged

    Solution:

    1. Confirm whether the damage is caused by human action or a natural disaster. If confirmed, the user needs to purchase the relevant parts again.
    2. If the damage is not caused by human action or a natural disaster, ask about the cause of the damage and replace the vision module.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Wheel Exterior Part Detached

    Solution:

    1. Provide a clear photo of the back of the exterior part, and ask about how the damage occurred.
    2. If the damage is caused by human action, the user needs to purchase the part themselves. If it is not caused by human action, send the relevant part as a replacement.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Front Decorative Cover Damaged

    Solution:

    1. Provide a clear photo of the back of the exterior part, and ask about how the damage occurred.
    2. If the damage is caused by human action, the user needs to purchase the part themselves. If it is not caused by human action, send the relevant part as a replacement.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Shock Absorber Broken from Falling

    Solution:

    1. Provide a clear photo of the back of the exterior part, and ask about how the damage occurred.
    2. If the damage is caused by human action, the user needs to purchase the part themselves. If it is not caused by human action, send the relevant part as a replacement.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Garage Missing Parts

    Solution:

    1. Take and provide photos of all components, confirm whether the outer packaging was damaged upon receipt, and directly send a replacement garage.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - RTK Mounting Bracket Structure Abnormal

    Solution:

    1. Take and provide clear photos of the abnormal area.
    2. Confirm whether it was damaged upon receipt, or was hit by the mower during operation.
    3. If it was damaged upon receipt, send a new RTK pole assembly. If it was damaged during operation, still send a replacement, but it is recommended to install the RTK in a high position (open area, open sky view) when reinstalling, rather than directly on the lawn.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Exterior Part Yellowing

    Solution:

    1. Collect photos of the yellowing, and verify the yellowing issue.
    2. Send a replacement exterior part.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Missing Cables / Accessories

    Solution:

    1. Determine the missing parts based on the photo / video / indicator information.
    2. Arrange to send the missing parts as replacements.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Garage Screws Damaged / Holes Misaligned

    Solution:

    1. Collect photos of the abnormal garage, and verify whether the garage screws are damaged or the holes are misaligned.
    2. Send a replacement garage (no individual parts will be sent separately).

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Charging Station / Contact Pin Stuck

    Solution:

    1. Collect information about the abnormal charging station, and verify that the contact strip is stuck.
    2. Send a replacement charging station.

    If not solved: Needs Repair

    Missing/Damaged/Defective Parts - Charging Station Mounting Hole Damaged (High Wind)

    Solution:

    1. Collect information about the abnormal charging station, and verify that the contact strip is stuck.
    2. Send a replacement charging station.

    If not solved: Needs Repair
