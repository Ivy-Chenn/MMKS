    Auto-reverse Mowing Direction
    		 It will automatically and randomly switch the operation directions of internal mowing and edge trimming, without repeatedly rolling the lawn in the same direction every time. This is mainly designed to reduce lawn lodging caused by long-term repeated rolling in the same direction. However, deep pits in front of the charging station cannot be prevented by this function.
