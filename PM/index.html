    📦 Product Info & Setup

    Overview

    This section provides essential product knowledge and setup guidance for Mammotion's distributors, dealers, and authorized service partners.

    It covers product specifications, model comparisons, positioning and cutting technologies, hardware components, installation requirements, and App functions.

    The resources are designed to help partners understand product capabilities, recommend suitable models, guide customers through initial setup, and provide accurate product information.

    💡 User Tips

    Start with Customer Requirements

    Before recommending a product, consider lawn size, terrain, slopes, obstacles, positioning conditions, and expected mowing performance.

    Compare Product Generations

    Product specifications, hardware configurations, and supported functions may differ between models and generations. Always verify the exact product version.

    Check Installation Conditions

    Review the installation checklist and positioning requirements before guiding customers through setup.

    Use Product Knowledge for Customer Support

    Refer to product specifications, component information, and App functions when answering customer questions or providing operational guidance.

    Knowledge Scope

    This section primarily supports product knowledge, sales consultation, installation preparation, and basic operational guidance. For fault diagnosis and issue resolution, refer to the Troubleshooting section.
